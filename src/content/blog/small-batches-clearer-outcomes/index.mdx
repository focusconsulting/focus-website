Large plans can create the appearance of certainty while hiding the decisions
that matter most. Working in small batches makes those decisions visible.

## Make progress observable

A useful increment gives a team something concrete to evaluate with users,
program staff, and technical partners. Instead of asking whether a plan seems
complete, the team can ask whether the service made a real task easier.

That shift improves more than delivery speed. It creates a shared language for
tradeoffs, exposes dependencies earlier, and makes it easier to change course
without discarding months of work.

## Keep the outcome larger than the release

Small batches are not small ambitions. Each increment should connect to a
durable outcome and leave the system easier to change than it was before. The
team moves deliberately, while evidence—not ceremony—sets the pace.
