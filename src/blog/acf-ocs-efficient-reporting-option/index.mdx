Focus is excited to [continue our partnership](https://orangeslices.ai/focus-awarded-hhs-acf-community-services-forms-engine-for-efficient-reporting-contract/) with the HHS Administration for Children and Families (ACF) to implement the Community Services Forms Engine for Efficient Reporting. Initial development will focus on launching a flexible, cloud-hosted platform featuring dynamic form builders, automated validation pipelines, and integrated data analytics to streamline federal grant reporting and compliance.

The Office of Community Services (OCS), an office within HHS ACF, works in partnership with states, tribes, territories, and community-based organizations to reduce poverty, revitalize low-income communities, and empower individuals and families to achieve economic self-sufficiency. OCS aligns federal resources and manages vital grant programs to build strong, resilient communities across the country.

Focus is a digital services firm that bridges the gap between industry knowledge and government services. This solution builds on Focus’s deep expertise implementing and modernizing benefits administration, forms automation, and digital adjudication systems across state and federal human services programs. And together we’re excited to collaborate on an essential tool to enable and support federal grantees in efficiently delivering impact to communities across the nation.
