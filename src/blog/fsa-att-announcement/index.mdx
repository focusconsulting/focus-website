Focus is delighted to announce that our joint venture with Nava has been [awarded a 5 year prime contract](https://orangeslices.ai/nava-and-focus-jv-beats-out-3-to-win-42m-dept-of-education-federal-student-aid-automated-testing-teams-att-contract/) with the U.S. Department of Education's Office of Federal Student Aid to deliver automated testing services. Together we will extend and improve modern automated testing frameworks, continuous release validation pipelines, and automated accessibility and security checks across FSA's core financial aid digital platforms in order to ensure robustness and the highest level of service to the tens of millions of Americans benefiting from FSA programs.

The Office of Federal Student Aid is the largest provider of student financial aid in the United States, delivering more than $120 billion in grant, work-study, and loan assistance annually. FSA manages a federal student loan portfolio exceeding $1.6 trillion and serves over 40 million students and borrowers nationwide, providing vital financial support and digital tools to help individuals pursue postsecondary education.

Focus is a digital services firm that bridges the gap between industry knowledge and government services. This solution builds on Focus’s deep expertise modernizing complex federal IT infrastructure, implementing agile software quality engineering, and establishing resilient automated test pipelines across critical public-facing systems. And together with Nava, we’re excited to collaborate on an essential effort to strengthen system reliability and empower millions of students accessing higher education benefits.
